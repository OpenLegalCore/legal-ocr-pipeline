# OpenLegalCore community code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the OpenLegalCore community welcoming, safe,
and equitable. Everyone who participates in good faith and follows this code is
entitled to respectful treatment.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate kindly, honestly, and professionally;
- respect different viewpoints, backgrounds, and levels of experience;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- take responsibility for their words, actions, and contributions;
- protect confidential, personal, legally privileged, and security-sensitive
  information;
- credit sources and disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- focus discussion on the project's technical and community purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
threats, impersonation, repeated unwanted contact, deliberate disruption, and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. So are
retaliation against a reporter and the use of private legal documents or OCR
content to make a public point without authorization.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other OpenLegalCore community spaces. It also applies when someone officially
represents the project in public or private settings.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report an incident privately through the repository's [GitHub security advisory
channel](https://github.com/OpenLegalCore/legal-ocr-pipeline/security/advisories/new)
and state that it is a code-of-conduct report. If that form is unavailable,
follow the fallback in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) without disclosing incident
details publicly.

Maintainers will prioritize reporter safety and confidentiality, review available
evidence, hear the involved parties where appropriate, and apply proportionate
measures. Measures can include clarification, a private warning, limits on
participation, temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may
skip intermediate steps. Retaliation is itself a violation.
